Tolerance and reset

    DeCaf

    Learning about you

    Rate how alert you feel, and the model learns your own half-life and sensitivity. Bayes' rule on a grid of 400 guesses: each guess's probability is its prior (close to your settings) times how well it predicts your ratings. Ratings a few hours after coffee teach it the most, because half-life shows in how fast caffeine wears off.

    Your sleep

    –

    Your usual night. It fills in your sleep calendar and sets tonight's bedtime for the sleep check. Caffeine left at bedtime hurts your sleep, and how much you sleep decides how tired you feel.

    Your body

    Weight: caffeine spreads through about 0.6 L of body water per kg, so lighter people reach a higher level. Half-life: hours for your liver to clear half of it. DeCaf starts from the average, 5 h, and learns yours from your ratings. Smoking about halves it; birth control pills about double it.

    Your habit

    Daily caffeine builds tolerance, so this decides how strong each cup still feels. A coffee is about 95 mg, an espresso 63 mg, a Red Bull 80 mg. Receptors take a few weeks to fully adjust, then stay put.
