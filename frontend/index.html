    Learn

    Rate how alert you feel, and DeCaf learns your own half-life and sensitivity.

    Bayes' rule on a grid of 400 guesses: each guess's probability is its prior (close to your settings) times how well it predicts your ratings. Ratings a few hours after coffee teach it the most, because half-life shows in how fast caffeine wears off.

    Your sleep

    –

    Caffeine left at bedtime hurts your sleep, and how much you sleep decides how tired you feel.

    Your habit

    Daily caffeine builds tolerance, so this decides how strong each cup still feels. A drip coffee is about 95 mg, an espresso 63 mg, a Red Bull 80 mg.

    Your body

    Caffeine spreads through about 0.6 L of body water per kg, so lighter people reach a higher level. Smoking about halves the half-life; birth control pills about double it.
