    Learning about you

    Rate how alert you feel, and the model learns your own half-life and sensitivity.

    Bayes' rule on a grid of 400 guesses: each guess's probability is its prior (close to your settings) times how well it predicts your ratings. Ratings a few hours after coffee teach it the most, because half-life shows in how fast caffeine wears off.

    Your body

    Weight: caffeine spreads through about 0.6 L of body water per kg, so lighter people reach a higher level. Half-life: hours for your liver to clear half of it, about 5 h on average and 2.5 to 10 h across people. Smoking about halves it; the pill about doubles it.

    Your habit

    Daily caffeine builds tolerance, so this decides how strong each cup still feels. A drip coffee is about 95 mg, an espresso 63 mg, a Red Bull 80 mg. Receptors take a few weeks to fully adjust, then stay put.
